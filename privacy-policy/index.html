This Privacy Policy describes how VRAM Lab (vramlab.com, the “Site”) handles information when you visit.

Information We Collect

The Site is a static website. We do not require accounts, and we do not directly collect personal information from visitors.

The Site may use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand overall traffic. This does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors.

Advertising

We may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • For more information on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Affiliate Links

Some posts contain affiliate links. If you purchase a product or service through such a link, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Posts containing affiliate links are identified as such. Our measurements and conclusions are not influenced by affiliate relationships — negative results are published regardless.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set cookies. Third-party services embedded in pages (such as advertising described above, or comment widgets) may set their own cookies subject to their own policies.

External Links

Posts link to external sites (documentation, GitHub issues, forums). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Site adds features (for example, comments or advertising). The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see Contact.
